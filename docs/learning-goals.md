## Jogo dos Pares

- **Objectives:** Practice sustaining attention while looking across the two card groups, reasoning about object relationships, and noticing rhyming sounds.
- **Mechanics:** Each theme has five different rounds of six pairs. The child matches related pictures or rhyming words by dragging or selecting one card from each side. Naming the match makes the relationship explicit; a brief visual confirmation and optional gentle sound acknowledge the match. Incorrect choices do not remove progress, so the child can keep trying.
- **Themes:** Princesses, home, animals, transport, food, garden, and rhyming words.
- **Difficulty:** One fixed level with six pairs (12 cards). The selected theme stays the same through the session, while each round uses a different set of pairings.

## O que está acontecendo?

- **Objectives:** Practice interpreting everyday scenes, associating pictures and written phrases, reading, and vocabulary.
- **Mechanics:** Choose a theme, then complete its five illustrated situations in sequence. Choose one of three phrases per situation. Mistakes can be retried without losing progress; a gentle clue appears after three incorrect attempts. After the fifth exercise the session ends with a completion sound and a congratulations screen that returns to the theme chooser.
- **Difficulty:** One fixed level: five exercises per theme, one theme per session. The 3/4/5 round setting does not apply to this game.
- **Themes:** Animais, Casa, Escola, Comidas, Parque, and Transportes.

The parent can confirm whether five exercises per theme and the theme list feel comfortable for the child, and whether the vocabulary (for example "alimentando", "estábulo", "galinheiro") is familiar.

## Jogo das Emoções

- **Objectives:** Practice noticing facial-expression cues and connecting familiar situations with possible feelings.
- **Mechanics:** Choose one of three levels: compare two faces for a matching emotion, name an emotion from a face, or connect a situation with a feeling. Each five-question round uses a random, non-repeating selection from that level. A short spoken explanation follows each correct answer; mistakes remain retryable and receive a gentle clue after three attempts.
- **Difficulty:** The face levels begin with feliz, triste, com raiva, and com medo. Situations use familiar, concrete events. Each level can grow by adding questions to `content.json`, without changing game code.

These are practice opportunities, not a measure or diagnosis of the child's emotional understanding. The parent can confirm whether the expressions, situations, and words feel familiar and comfortable.

## Coloque em Ordem

- **Objectives:** Practice reading and building grammatical Brazilian Portuguese sentences, noticing word order, and connecting written phrases to a familiar image.
- **Mechanics:** Choose a level with three, four, or five words. Each five-question round samples without repeats from `content.json`. Drag a word to a numbered space or tap it to fill the next empty space. A misplaced word returns to the bank with gentle feedback; correctly placed words stay locked. Completing the sentence reveals and optionally reads the full sentence and a short explanation.
- **Difficulty:** Sentences grow from three to five word tokens. They use familiar subjects and actions with concise, concrete vocabulary. Content validation checks sentence length, token order, duplicate tokens, local image references, and explanations.

These are practice opportunities, not a measure of the child's ability. The parent can confirm whether the vocabulary, reading level, and sentence length feel familiar and comfortable.
